# HTML5 Elements Reference

Use this as a quick reference while building your elements page. Categories are based on MDN's organization.

## Sectioning & Structure
`<html>`, `<head>`, `<body>`, `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<aside>`, `<footer>`

## Text Content
`<h1>` through `<h6>`, `<p>`, `<blockquote>`, `<hr>`, `<pre>`, `<div>`, `<ul>`, `<ol>`, `<li>`, `<dl>`, `<dt>`, `<dd>`

## Inline Text
`<a>`, `<strong>`, `<em>`, `<code>`, `<kbd>`, `<samp>`, `<var>`, `<sub>`, `<sup>`, `<span>`, `<br>`, `<b>`, `<i>`, `<u>`, `<mark>`, `<small>`

## Embedded Content & Media
`<img>`, `<video>`, `<audio>`, `<source>`, `<picture>`, `<canvas>`, `<svg>`, `<iframe>`, `<embed>`, `<object>`

## Forms
`<form>`, `<label>`, `<input>`, `<button>`, `<select>`, `<datalist>`, `<option>`, `<textarea>`, `<fieldset>`, `<legend>`

## Table
`<table>`, `<caption>`, `<thead>`, `<tbody>`, `<tfoot>`, `<tr>`, `<th>`, `<td>`, `<col>`, `<colgroup>`

## Metadata & Head Elements
`<title>`, `<meta>`, `<link>`, `<style>`, `<script>`, `<noscript>`, `<base>`

## Semantic/Emphasis
`<time>`, `<address>`, `<cite>`, `<q>`, `<dfn>`, `<ins>`, `<del>`, `<s>`, `<wbr>`

---

**Total:** ~90–100 core elements. You don't need to list every single one — focus on the most common and useful ones, organized by category.

**Tip:** You can organize your page in two ways:
1. **Table format** — columns for Element, Purpose, Example, Self-closing
2. **Grouped sections** — use `<section>` or `<article>` for each category with nested lists
